### Top 5 findings by impact, and their fixes

Ranked by what each one does to real money or real accounts. All five are Critical. Four have a production incident or production evidence behind them. Each fix is one commit with a test that failed before the fix and passes after it. The full entries are in section 4.

| # | Issue | Evidence | Fix | Proof | Commit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Merchant login never needed the OTP (02).** The cookie issued after the password step was already a full session. A stolen password was enough to take over a merchant's dashboard and API. | The session view `live_sessions` (0058) dropped the `authenticated = true` filter that 0012 requires. The doc comment claiming otherwise was false. Brief lead: "login works without entering the OTP". | One line: `lookupSession` requires `AND s.authenticated = true`. | Password-only cookie on `/v1/auth/me` and `/v1/transactions`: was 200, now 401. The same cookie works after OTP verify. 6 tests, 3 failed before. | `0685a87` |
| 2 | **Refunds paid out money that wasn't the merchant's (26, 27, 28).** Merchant B could refund merchant A's sale. A refund could exceed the captured amount. Two refunds sent at once both paid. | Lookups matched the transaction id only. The ceiling was *authorized − refunded*. Phase 1 read without a lock, then wrote an absolute `refunded_fils`. Brief ticket FP-209. | Scope both lookups by the authenticated merchant (404 otherwise). The ceiling is *captured − refunded*, enforced by a DB `CHECK` (0076). The refund runs in one row-locked transaction that adds to `refunded_fils`. | B → A's sale: refund succeeded before, 404 and nothing moves now. Five simultaneous 3,000-fil refunds of a 10,000 capture: all 5 paid before, at most 3 now. Two full refunds: exactly one. | `71a8ebe`, `cb044bc`, `d9d4f30` |
| 3 | **One payment link charged two buyers (23).** Incident 2026-05-14: a slow payment let a second buyer re-claim the link, and both cards were captured. | The reservation had no owner, and a stale one could be re-claimed. Finalize matching 0 rows still answered "paid". One buyer's release could reopen another's reservation. The CHANGELOG's "callback dedup" fix was unrelated. | Each claim gets a `reservation_id` (0075). The link is marked paid atomically, and only by the holder, *before* capture. The loser's authorization is voided and gets 409. | Forced overlap test: two captures and two 200s before. One capture, 200 + 409 and the loser's authorization voided now. 28 link tests pass. | `351cab5` |
| 4 | **Payouts recorded and announced more than once (24).** Incident 2026-08-21. A disbursement row is a payout instruction, and duplicates are duplicate payouts. Merchants with no webhook endpoint got none. | `settlement.paid` was inserted after a `NOT EXISTS` check with no unique key, so concurrent runs both insert. The webhook dispatcher wrote one disbursement per *delivery* (per endpoint, per replay). | The disbursement is written inside `PayoutBatch`, under the batch lock, exactly once per paid batch. There is one `settlement.paid` per batch. | Before: 4 events per batch from four concurrent runs, 2 disbursements from the dispatcher, 0 at payout. Now: exactly one of each. | `c0f764d` |
| 5 | **CSV ledger adjustments posted at a tenth of their value (13).** "4.75" BD became 0.475 BD, and a third decimal was dropped. | Brief's finance memo (4.755 → 4.750; 4.75 → 0.475). Slack 27 Aug: a 4,141.90 BD sheet posted 414.190 BD. The parser treated amounts as cents (2 decimals), against ADR-0001. | Parse with `parseBhdToFils` (3 decimals, rejects more). | 16 tests: 12 failed before (414,190 instead of 4,141,900 fils), all pass now. | `c797270` |
